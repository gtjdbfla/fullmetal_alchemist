# Core Rules

## Priority
1. Hard rules
2. Latest Story Bible structured data (`private/dashboard-config.json` → `storyBible`)
3. Latest approved manuscript
4. Older notes and drafts

A conflict between an approved manuscript and Story Bible is flagged, never silently rewritten.

## Absolute rules
- Edward never regains alchemy.
- Ishvala is never confirmed as Truth.
- Red mineral is not magical, not a Philosopher's Stone equivalent, and does not exist in Karsa.
- Al has no direct POV.
- Kairun is not a Bai agent; Jenha never orders assassination.
- Graus plans a brief real war from the start; Bark does not directly kill civilians after T0.
- The final political answer is not instant perfect democracy or benevolent unilateral rule.

## T0: Bark order boundary
T0 is Graus's explicit Chapter 32 order: “앞으로 민간인은 죽이지 마.” Before T0, Bark's cell may commit intentional direct civilian shootings. After T0, intentional direct civilian killings by Bark or the cell are zero; later casualties arise only from the organically expanding conflict.
